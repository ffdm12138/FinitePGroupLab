# P02 — Exact-prime-order non-inner automorphisms

**Directory status:** publication index only; no P02 result is included yet.

The full target concerns every prime `p` and every finite nonabelian
`p`-group `G`, with `α : Aut(G)`, exact `orderOf α = p`, and
`α` outside the entire image of inner automorphisms of **the same G**.

A verified local cohomology lemma, subgroup case, or conditional construction
may be published as a **local lemma**, never as an unconditional P02 solution.
A full-solution claim additionally needs a top-level kernel proof, independent
source-to-Lean semantic equivalence, complete dependency review and formal
Main acceptance at the **same** version, plus current literature checking.
